# Introduction

## Efficiency of Algorithms

Efficiency is judged based on two parameters,
1. The space taken by the algorithm to store variables, constants during run-time. It is known as **Space Complexity** of the algorithm.
2. The time taken by the algorithm to complete its execution. It is known as **Time Complexity** of the algorithm.

> **Note:** No complexity gives the exact answer but it gives boundary i.e., the highest possible value, lowest possible value or average value.
