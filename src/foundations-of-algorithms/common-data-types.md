# Common Data-types

As discussed [earlier](./elements-of-algorithmic-notation.md#useful-terminologies), that data-types are rarely explicitly mentioned within algorithms. From context, the data-type of a variable or constant is inferred. Though often, it's not mentioned explicitly in algorithms, it's important to understand what data-types are and in which contexts which data-type is suitable.

## Different kinds of Data-types

Commonly data-types can be categorized into two categories,
1. **Primitive Data-type:** It is a single-valued unbreakable data-type.

    **Examples:** Numeric (integer, real), Characters, and Booleans are primitive data-types
2. **Composite Data-type:** The data-types that are made up of Primitive Data-types are called Composite Data-type.

    **Examples:** String, Array, Linked List etc.
